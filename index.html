Varad Pimpalkhute

वरद पिंपळखुटे · V-uh-r-uh-d

I work on the science and systems of reinforcement learning for LLM agents.

I'm a Research Scientist at the Institute of Foundation Models (MBZUAI) in Sunnyvale, where I lead post-training RL research for reasoning, software-engineering, terminal, and deep-research agents.

I led RL360, IFM's agentic RL infrastructure, which trained K2 Horizon's domain experts, and I led coding-agent task development and post-training for K2 Horizon. I built and scaled our asynchronous RL infrastructure, improving throughput 4× and stabilizing post-training across 400B MoE and 70B dense models on 1000s of GPUs. I've been a core post-training contributor on every K2 release and co-led the K2-Think-V2 release. I also study how RL compute should be spent (IsoCompute), where I mentored the interns behind the work.

Before IFM, I was at Articul8 (where I trained an 8B Verilog code model end to end), AI2, Amazon, Cedars-Sinai (RADS Scholar), and TCS Research. I did my MS in Computer Science at UMass Amherst and my BTech at IIIT Nagpur.

Research

I'm interested in how RL post-training scales: how to spend compute, how to build systems that keep thousands of agents learning, and how to ship it in open frontier models. Highlighted work is shaded.

IsoCompute Playbook: Optimally Scaling Sampling Compute for LLM RL
Zhoujun Cheng, Yutao Xie, Yuxiao Qu, Amrith Setlur, Shibo Hao, Varad Pimpalkhute, Tongtong Liang, Feng Yao, Zhengzhong Liu, Eric Xing, Virginia Smith, Ruslan Salakhutdinov, Zhiting Hu, Taylor Killian, Aviral Kumar
arXiv, 2026  ·  playbook / arXiv / thread

The compute-optimal number of rollouts per problem grows predictably with budget, then saturates. Extra rollouts sharpen the policy on easy problems and expand coverage on hard ones.

RL360: Agentic RL Infrastructure for K2 HorizonLEAD
Institute of Foundation Models, 2026  ·  code / W&B run / thread

Open-source agentic RL at scale: 8,192 concurrent sandboxes and about 1.3M rollouts in 7 days. Rollouts, sandboxes, inference, and training scale independently, and infra failures are masked out of the loss instead of being scored as wrong answers.

K2 Horizon: Frontier Performance, Radically OpenCODING-AGENT POST-TRAINING LEAD
Institute of Foundation Models, 2026  ·  blog / models

Six fully open models, up to 375B-A23B, released with weights, data, code, and logs. I led coding-agent task development and post-training (SWE and terminal environments, rollout and evaluation pipelines), and RL360 trained its domain experts.

Unlocking Lossless Speedups in LLMs via Discrete Diffusion
Subham Sekhar Sahoo, Lingjie Chen, Khiem Pham, Jonathan Geuter, Chaitanya Dwivedi, Varad Pimpalkhute, Yash Akhauri, Alexander Moreno, Mikhail Yurochkin, Zhenting Wang, Mostafa Elhoushi, Nolan Dey, Shane Bergsma, Joel Hestness, John Thickstun, Eric Xing, Zhengzhong Liu
arXiv, 2026  ·  project page / arXiv

UNO adds lightweight diffusion weights to an autoregressive LLM to decode several tokens at once: up to 3× faster generation with the base model's distribution preserved, and no draft model.

K2-V2: A 360-Open, Reasoning-Enhanced LLMCORE CONTRIBUTOR
Institute of Foundation Models, including Varad Pimpalkhute
arXiv, 2025  ·  arXiv

A fully open, reasoning-enhanced LLM, released with its data, code, and training logs. I worked on post-training and evaluation, and co-led the K2-Think-V2 technical blog and release.

K2-Think: A Parameter-Efficient Reasoning SystemCORE CONTRIBUTOR
Z. Cheng, R. Fan, S. Hao, T. W. Killian, H. Li, S. Sun, H. Ren, A. Moreno, … Varad Pimpalkhute, …
arXiv, 2025  ·  arXiv

A 32B reasoning system that outperforms larger open-weight models across reasoning benchmarks through optimized post-training and inference.

Efficient Agentic Reasoning through Self-Regulated Simulative Planning
M. Deng, J. Hou, L. S. Neves, Varad Pimpalkhute, T. W. Killian, Z. Liu, E. P. Xing
arXiv, 2026  ·  arXiv

An agent that learns when and how deeply to plan, using simulated look-ahead only where it pays off.

SoftQE: Learned Representations of Queries Expanded by LLMs
Varad Pimpalkhute, J. Heyer, X. Yin, S. Gupta
ECIR, 2024  ·  arXiv / Amazon Science

A query encoder that distills LLM query expansion into the embedding itself: better dense retrieval (+2.8%) with no added latency.
